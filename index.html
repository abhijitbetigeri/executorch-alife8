DCSM-Simplified

DataCenter Service Management, Simplified

A handheld for the data-center floor that runs fully offline on the Alif Ensemble E8. Point the camera at a component and the Ethos-U85 NPU names it. Then your voice opens an inspection checklist and the part’s specs, troubleshooting and LED codes. No phone, no network, no cloud.

How it works

Point. Hold. Inspect.

Secure data-center floors ban phones, cameras and cloud. Technicians still have to identify parts they rarely touch, with their hands busy in a rack.

01 · camera

Point at a part

A MobileNetV2 on the NPU names it with a confidence and an info card. When it is unsure, it shows nothing instead of guessing.

02 · say “go”

Hold and check

The part is held and an inspection checklist opens. “Yes” and “no” mark each item; “no” on item 1 rejects a wrong identification.

03 · say “go” again

Look it up

Specs, troubleshooting, LED codes and maintenance for that exact part, retrieved on the device.

On-device retrieval, keyed by what the camera sees

The query is the identified class plus a voice command. A 15 KB knowledge base for all 16 parts is compiled into the firmware.

Fan · Troubleshooting (2/4)
High noise: check for obstructions or failed bearing.
All fans high speed: check ambient temperature or blocked airflow.

Why retrieval, not a generating LLM

  • About 195 KB of memory is left after the two models; even a tiny language model needs about 15 MB.
  • On a 400 MHz Cortex-M55 it would take seconds per word.
  • The voice model knows 10 words, not sentences.
  • Every answer comes from the knowledge base, never invented.

DCSM-Simplified · 06

Decisions

Six choices, each one measured.

Classifier, not detector

Vela scrambled a detector’s outputs on the board. A classifier runs 100% on the NPU and matches the PC.

Distil from a teacher

91.3% on board-like images with distillation, 80.4% without it.

160 px input

Best of 128, 160 and 192 px in a sweep, at the same model size.

Camera layout in

NHWC input, normalisation folded into the model: no reshuffling on the CPU.

Blank beats wrong

Smoothing over frames plus a 50% gate: 6% wrong labels, the rest correct or blank.

5 demo classes of 16

The ones the board camera separates reliably, picked from held-out data.

Where this goes

From rack parts to the parts on the board

Today DCSM-Simplified tells a technician which unit to pull or check. The real servicing win is one level down: which part on the board failed, like a swollen capacitor, a burnt chip or a loose connector, with its specs and the fix, still offline.
